The Composition

A Scientific Symphony

At NOIRÉ, we view the creation of a perfume not as a commercial endeavor, but as an architectural feat. We begin with a skeletal structure of woody base notes, layering heart notes of rare flora, and finishing with volatile, sparkling top notes that command immediate intrigue.

Our ingredients are sourced from private estates across five continents—ensuring uncompromising quality and unmatched longevity on skin and fabric.

The Flacon

Craftsmanship in Crystal

We believe that a masterpiece should be housed in a vessel that reflects its gravity. Each NOIRÉ flacon is hand-blown from heavyweight lead-free crystal and topped with a cold-pressed metallic cap, gold-plated to prevent oxidation.

The dark tint of our glass serves as a shield against ultraviolet rays, preserving the volatile molecular structure of the extraits within.

Ethical Sourcing

Every raw material is sourced via sustainable cooperatives, ensuring the longevity of these rare botanical species and fair treatment of harvesters.

Slow Extraction

We reject industrial flash-distillation. Our essences are extracted via cold maceration to retain the full vibrational profile of each flower and wood.

Concentrated Purity

While standard perfumes offer 15% concentration, every NOIRÉ release is an Extrait de Parfum at a minimum of 32% concentration.
